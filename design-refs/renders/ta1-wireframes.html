TA-1 wireframes, round 2

Populated faceplates built in the K.O. II manner and quoting the Braun references: the pocket cassette recorder, the icon set (audio 1, tuner 1, game one, the calculator, video 1) and the SK4. Screens are 4:3 or taller, never a wide band. One continuous warm-grey chassis, black or light keys with Rubik labels, one orange, a small wordmark bottom-left and the model name bottom-right the way Braun signs a face. Every control has a job.
