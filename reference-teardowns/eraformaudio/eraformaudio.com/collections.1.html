What does the XY Pad in the middle do?

Left and Right (X) blends between sound layer A and B while Up and Down (Y) is split into two sections: 

Up and down in the bottom half of the XY Pad, controls the dry and wet of the effects page (0 - 75%).
When you go from the bottom to the top half of the XY Pad, that is where the Filter turns on in the advanced page.

Up and down in the top half of the XY Pad, controls the strength of the LFOs being applied to the Filter.

My Expansions are not loading into Fraction.

99% of the time it is about subfolders. Your "Expansions" folder needs to look like this.

1. Download and unzip the 26GB Expansions file (store it anywhere on your computer).
2. Bottom left of the Plugin → Settings → Sample Path, and select the “Expansions” folder inside the Factory Content folder.

Restart the VST.

Important: The folder your locating must be exactly named “Expansions” for them to show up in the preset browser.

The "Expansions" folder is also where any of your new Expansions are going into like Monuments or Core-Synth. But make sure there are no subfolders otherwise it won't show up.

Fraction won't show up in my DAW.

Run the correct installer for your operating system and the Fraction VST file will go to these places: 

WIN: C:/Program Files/Common Files/VST3 

MAC: C:/Program Files/Common Files/Components

and then make sure your DAW scans these folders (even tho it already does that per default).

Compatibility
What DAWs are supported and what are the system requirements?

Fraction works in any DAW that supports VST3 or AU formats, including FL Studio, Ableton Live, Logic Pro, Studio One,
Reaper, GarageBand and more...

It works on macOS 10.9+ and Windows 10+
