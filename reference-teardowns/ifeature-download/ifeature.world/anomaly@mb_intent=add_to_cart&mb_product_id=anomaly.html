WHAT IS SHIFT IN ANOMALY?

Frequency Shift (Coarse)
Range: -10,000 Hz to +10,000 Hz
Controls the main frequency shifting amount. Positive values shift frequencies upward, negative values shift them downward.

WHAT IS FINE IN ANOMALY?

Frequency Shift (Fine)
Range: -500Hz to +500Hz
Allows precise adjustment of the shift amount.

WHAT IS SPREAD IN ANOMALY?

Wide (Button)
Enables stereo spreading.
Spread (Knob)
Controls the stereo width of the band by offsetting frequency shift between the left and right channels.

WHAT IS MIX IN ANOMALY?

Mix (Dry/Wet)
Range: 0% to 100%
Blends between the unprocessed (dry) signal and the frequency-shifted (wet) signal.

WHAT IS MIDI KEYTRACK IN ANOMALY?

MIDI Keytrack
Range: C-1 to C7
Uses incoming MIDI notes modulate the frequency shift amount. Higher notes result in higher shift values, while lower notes produce lower shift values.

WHAT ARE BANDS IN ANOMALY?

ANOMALY splits the incoming audio signal into up to three frequency bands using crossover filters.

The frequency shifter is applied exclusively to the middle band. The low and high bands pass through unprocessed. Each band can be soloed or removed. If only one band remains, the frequency shifter remains active on the full signal.

The crossover slope can be adjusted to control how sharply the frequency ranges are separated.

WHAT IS THE SPECTRUM DISPLAY?

Spectrum Display Visualizes the frequency content of the input signal and shows how it is being affected by ANOMALY's processing

WHAT IS ECO IN ANOMALY?

Eco Mode reduces the Display processing quality to lower CPU usage
