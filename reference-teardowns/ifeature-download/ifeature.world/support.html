FAQ

Is there a free trial?
Yes. All plugins include a fully functional 3-day trial with no feature limitations.
How does activation work?
After purchase, your plugin unlocks instantly with automatic activation. No license keys required.
How many devices can I activate?
Each license can be activated on one computer at a time. You can deactivate and transfer your license if you switch devices.
Which formats and systems are supported?
All plugins are available in VST3 and AU formats for macOS and Windows.
Will I receive updates?
Yes. All minor updates are included with your purchase.
What if the plugin doesn’t appear in my DAW?
Make sure your DAW scans the correct plugin folder. If the issue persists, contact support and we’ll assist you quickly.
